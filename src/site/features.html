Everything aplyx does for your job search

One local-first pipeline, from finding postings to a reviewable, ready-to-send application.

Multi-board search

Ashby, Lever, Greenhouse, Workday, SmartRecruiters, and more, searched in one pass and deduplicated automatically, so the same posting never shows up twice just because it's listed on two boards. Every result is fit-checked against your preferences right in the same view, so you can act without switching screens.

A deterministic fit gate

Every posting is checked against your role, level, and location preferences with plain, rule-based logic before anything reaches a resume-tailoring step, not a model guessing whether a job is a fit.

Resume & cover letter tailoring

Keep one resume in aplyx: jobs, projects, skills, education, entered once. Bullets are rewritten and reordered per job description, and a short cover letter is drafted grounded strictly in your actual resume and the posting, never invented experience.

Review before it goes out

Anything uncertain lands in a review queue. You decide before it's submitted. Nothing is applied to on your behalf without a decision you control. Each entry shows its ATS score, the resume picked for it, and the fit-gate reasoning behind it, so a decision never means guessing.

Status tracking, straight from your inbox Work in progress

Once an application goes out, aplyx watches the inbox on your profile for what happens next (a rejection, an assessment, an interview request, an offer) and updates that job's status automatically. Read-only, opt-in, off by default.

✓ Applied ▤ OA sent ◆ Interview requested ★ Offer ✗ Rejected

TUI and desktop, same core

A terminal app for a fast, keyboard-first workflow, and a native desktop app for the same thing visually, both driven by the same local-first pipeline, so neither is a second-class surface.

Discord updates, optional

Get pinged as applications go out, need review, or fail, entirely opt-in and off by default.
