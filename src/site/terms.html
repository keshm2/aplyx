The short version

These terms cover everything under the aplyx name: the free command-line tool and desktop app, the browser extension, this website, a free hosted account, and the paid hosted plans that aren't live yet. Using any of it means you accept these terms.

Who can use aplyx

You need to be at least 16 and old enough to enter a binding agreement where you live. One account per person. Don't share your login.

What aplyx does, and what it doesn't

aplyx searches job boards, checks each posting against your profile with fixed rules, tailors your resume per posting, and can fill out and submit applications for you. Anything uncertain lands in a review queue for you to decide on. aplyx is a tool that acts on your instructions. It does not find you a job, guarantee interviews or offers, or promise that any given application was received, is error-free, or went out on time.

You're responsible for what goes out under your name

Every application aplyx submits carries your name to a real employer. You are confirming that the profile, resume, and answers you give aplyx are accurate and truthful, that you're allowed to apply to the roles you target, and that you authorize aplyx to submit on your behalf. Don't use aplyx to lie about your qualifications, spam employers, apply for someone else, or break the law.

Job boards and ATS platforms have their own rules

aplyx interacts with third-party sites (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, and others) and, if you add them, your own login credentials for those sites. Automated access can conflict with a platform's own terms, and a platform can rate-limit, flag, suspend, or ban an account for it. You accept that risk. aplyx isn't responsible for a third-party platform's decisions, downtime, or changes that break a feature.

Your coding agent

The local tool drives a coding agent you install and pay for yourself (opencode, Claude Code, and others). That agent and its model provider have their own terms and their own costs, which are between you and them.

Paying for a plan

Paid plans are monthly subscriptions that renew automatically until you cancel. Cancel any time from your account. No refunds once a payment is made. If you cancel before your next billing date, you keep full access to the tier you paid for until the end of that paid period, then the account drops to the free tier. Prices can change with notice before your next renewal. You're responsible for any taxes.
