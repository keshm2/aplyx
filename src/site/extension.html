It notices the form before you do

The aplyx browser extension watches for a real application form on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, or Workday — not every page you visit — and asks before it does anything. Say yes, and it fills the form from your profile. You still review every field and click submit yourself.

How it works

Four steps, every time — nothing happens on a page until it's asked, and nothing it fills gets submitted without you.

  1. 1

    Detects

    Watches for a real, fillable application form on a supported board — stays invisible everywhere else, including job listing and search pages.

    Invisible until there's a form
  2. 2

    Asks

    A prompt drops down asking "Autofill this application with aplyx?" — a real yes/no, not an assumption.

    Permission first
  3. 3

    Fills

    Maps visible fields to your profile and fills only what it can answer honestly. Anything it can't is highlighted for you, never invented.

    Never invents a value
  4. 4

    You review & apply

    You check every field and click submit yourself. aplyx never clicks it for you — on any application, ever.

    You always click submit

Works on the boards you're already applying through
